Executive Summary
This document recommends a focused AI contact center proof of concept that demonstrates investor authentication and customer profile retrieval as a single foundational journey, followed by deal ticket and settlement inquiries delivered through consistent voice and chat experiences. The September 14 checkpoint confirmed that Statement Request is out of scope for the initial POC.
Meeting Decisions and Working Assumptions
• Statement Request is removed from the initial POC scope.
• The demo will use a single-account, straight-through scenario to limit complexity.
• Voice and chat will use a consistent authentication flow for the demo.
• GCX will host the demo interactions. Zilo scope is limited to authentication, authorization, and one sample test.
• The team will use a realistic mock or stub service with approved test data for the demo.
• Deal ticket and settlement journeys remain in scope; intent recognition, sentiment detection, smart routing, and live-agent handoff are included.
Priority 1: Investor Authentication & Customer Profile
Journey
Investor contacts the contact center via voice or chat.
AI agent greets the investor and initiates authentication.
Investor is authenticated using account number, registered contact details, and security validation.
Upon successful authentication, the AI agent retrieves the investor profile from Zilo.
Personalized customer context is established, enabling the agent to proceed with the requested service.
Why Important
Required before servicing any request.
Critical for both voice and chat channels.
Discussed extensively by the Google team as a prerequisite for all future workflows.
Dushyant Ralhan specifically identified retrieving the customer profile from Zilo as a minimum integration capability for the POC.
Desired Outcome
Authenticate investor.
Retrieve customer profile from Zilo (or mock service).
Establish personalized customer context.
Priority 2: Deal Ticket / Transaction Confirmation Request
Journey
Investor requests trade confirmation.
Agent identifies customer.
Trade information is retrieved.
Details are presented to investor.
Why Important
One of the explicit examples reviewed from the business documentation.
Priority 3: Settlement Inquiry / Receipt of Monies
Journey
Investor asks whether subscription funds or a wire transfer have been received and settled.
The agent authenticates the investor and identifies the relevant account or transaction.
The agent retrieves payment receipt, transaction, and settlement-status information.
The agent confirms whether the funds were received, matched, and settled, or explains the current exception status.
The request is escalated when the payment cannot be located, matched, or confirmed.
Why Important
Replaces the lower-value Contract / Note Inquiry journey.
Provides a more valuable transaction-servicing scenario with greater process complexity.
Demonstrates the agent’s ability to search across payment, subscription, bank-receipt, and settlement information.
Priority 4: Contact Center Voice Assistant
Journey
Investor calls support line.
AI voice agent greets caller.
Agent authenticates investor.
Agent routes directly to requested service.
Escalates to human agent when needed.
Why Important
Voice-first support was repeatedly discussed as part of the future-state vision.
Priority 5: Chat-Based Self-Service
Journey
Investor accesses portal.
Chat assistant provides self-service.
Customer receives answers or transaction support.
Escalation path exists when confidence is low.
Why Important
The business vision specifically called out voice and chat capabilities.
Priority 6: Intent Detection, Sentiment & Smart Routing
Journey
Investor contacts support.
AI determines the reason for inquiry and detects sentiment or frustration signals.
Appropriate workflow is selected automatically.
Request is fulfilled, routed, or transferred to a live agent when escalation criteria are met.
Why Important
Central to the Agentic AI model discussed by Google.

Demo Scenario An authenticated investor asks whether funds submitted for a subscription have been received and settled. The AI agent locates the relevant payment, confirms the receipt and matching status, and returns the settlement outcome. If the payment cannot be located or is pending investigation, the agent explains the status and initiates the approved escalation path.
Knowledge Articles Required
Top 10 Queries #7 – Receipt of Monies (current process: AWD, IFAST, Bank Rec). This procedure document should be loaded into the agent knowledge base as a guardrail source.
Required job aids: daily bank reconciliation file procedures, SUBNR outstanding monies file handling, settlement date plus 1 escalation rules, and cash-matching exception responses.
Escalation Conditions
Payment cannot be located in the bank rec file or Zilo transaction records.
Cash-matching exception, mismatch, or rejection is flagged on the transaction.
Settlement is at date-plus-1 and the transaction appears on the SUBNR outstanding file.
Wire transfer discrepancy or unidentified payment requires operations investigation.

	1. Investor Authentication & Customer Profile
Discussion Context The team identified authentication as a foundational capability, especially for phone interactions, and discussed how customers would authenticate before accessing account-specific information.
Success Criteria
✅ Investor identity can be validated.✅ Authentication workflow completes successfully.✅ AI can proceed to customer-specific servicing actions.✅ Authentication process works consistently across voice and chat channels.
Demo Outcome
Investor calls or starts a chat, authenticates successfully, and the AI agent immediately retrieves and confirms their profile from Zilo. The agent states: "I've verified your identity and pulled up your account. How can I help you today?"

2. Deal Ticket / Trade Inquiry
Discussion Context Deal ticket receipt was reviewed as one of the common customer service scenarios.
Success Criteria
✅ Investor requests deal information.✅ AI retrieves relevant trade details.✅ Trade data is presented accurately.✅ Conversation completes successfully without escalation.
Business Outcome
Shows ability to support transactional inquiries using customer-specific data.

3. Settlement Inquiry / Receipt of Monies
Discussion Context The stakeholders agreed to remove Contract / Note Inquiry because contract notes are expected to move toward digital self-service. Settlement inquiries and receipt-of-monies requests were selected as the replacement because they provide greater servicing value and demonstrate a more meaningful transaction-status workflow.
Success Criteria
✅ The investor can ask whether subscription funds or a wire transfer were received.✅ The AI agent identifies the correct account and transaction.✅ The AI agent retrieves the receipt-of-funds, matching, and settlement status.✅ The response accurately distinguishes received, matched, settled, pending, rejected, and not-found outcomes.✅ The agent follows the approved escalation process when the payment cannot be confirmed or requires investigation.✅ The conversation completes through voice or chat using the same underlying business rules.
Business Outcome
Demonstrates transaction-level servicing across payment receipt, cash matching, and settlement while reducing manual status inquiries handled by operations.

4. Voice AI Contact Center Experience
Discussion Context The future-state vision reviewed by the team included voice-first servicing capabilities.
Success Criteria
✅ Voice call received by AI agent.✅ Authentication completed.✅ Customer request understood correctly.✅ Appropriate workflow executed.✅ Response delivered naturally.✅ Escalation path available when required.
Demo Outcome
End-to-end voice conversation for a deal ticket or settlement inquiry.

5. Chat AI Experience
Discussion Context The reviewed vision documents referenced both voice and chat channels. Rob Cox also discussed handling channels independently while maintaining a single agent experience.
Success Criteria
✅ Customer initiates chat.✅ AI understands intent.✅ Customer serviced without agent involvement.✅ Consistent responses across voice and chat.
Demo Outcome
Investor requests a deal ticket or settlement inquiry via chat and receives a successful response.

6. Intent Recognition, Sentiment & Smart Routing
Discussion Context The Google team emphasized user journeys and workflow-driven servicing, where AI determines the customer's intent and selects the correct workflow.
Success Criteria
✅ AI identifies customer intent correctly.✅ Correct workflow selected automatically.✅ Customer receives expected outcome.✅ Escalation occurs when confidence is insufficient.

Executive-Level POC Success Criteria
The POC will be considered successful when it demonstrates functioning voice and chat channels, investor authentication, customer profile retrieval, the selected deal ticket and settlement journeys completed end-to-end, Zilo or realistic mock integration, AI-driven intent and sentiment recognition, workflow execution, and human-agent escalation when required.
✅ Voice and chat channels functioning.
✅ Investor authentication and customer profile retrieval.
✅ Deal ticket and settlement journeys working end-to-end.
✅ Integration with Zilo (or a realistic mock service).
✅ AI-driven intent and sentiment recognition, workflow execution, and live-agent handoff.
✅ Minimal human intervention for standard inquiries.
✅ Architecture that can scale to future retail investor volumes.
✅ Operational dashboard displaying call volumes, intent distribution, escalation rates, and handling times (GCX Insights or equivalent BI tooling).
These success criteria align with the stated business objectives, program direction, and proposed solution approach.
