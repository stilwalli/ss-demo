How Tara supports investor servicing

Tara combines conversational AI with Transfer Agency workflows to resolve common inquiries quickly while preserving appropriate authentication, routing, and escalation controls.

Secure investor authentication

Verifies account information and completes an authentication challenge before providing account-specific details.

Trade and transaction inquiries

Retrieves trade details such as transaction status, date, amount, and processing outcome. Tara can ask follow-up questions to help identify a transaction when the reference number is unavailable.

Receipt of monies

Checks whether subscription or payment funds have been received, matched, and settled, and explains the current transaction status.

Intelligent routing and escalation

Recognizes the investor’s intent and escalation signals, selects the appropriate servicing workflow, and connects the investor with a specialist when the request cannot be completed through self-service.

Conversation summaries

Creates a concise summary of the interaction so that context can be carried into follow-up workflows or provided to a servicing specialist.

Interaction insights

Captures conversation activity for analysis, including customer intent, interaction trends, handling information, and other operational insights available through the CX Insights experience.
